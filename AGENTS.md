# Topical Knowledge Cell Discovery Service

This settlement forms bounded freshness snapshots from an exact customer-supplied
denominator of signed manifest endpoints. It does not crawl a filesystem,
organizational hierarchy, Google account, ActivityPub network, or global
registry. Absence from the supplied denominator is unknown, never nonexistent.

Manifest identity, signed Agent Card discovery, and RWIL/RDF durability are
separately hired services. This node preserves stale, refused, and contradictory
entries; it cannot choose an authoritative Union view. Its results grant no
notebook creation, source mutation, sharing, deletion, publication, report, or
payment authority.
